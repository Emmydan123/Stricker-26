Landscape football · offline & online

Two files, no app store. index.html is the game. multiplayer.html is the join page your opponent opens. Everything is inside them — pitch, players, sound, and the online code.

Play on your phone

ANDROID
  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen — it launches full-screen, in landscape.
IPHONE
  1. Open the site in Safari.
  2. Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Turn the phone sideways — the game rotates itself and asks for landscape.

Online multiplayer — by code

1 · Friend: ONLINE → CREATE ROOM→2 · You: /multiplayer → type code→3 · Kick off
  • You create: main page → ONLINE → CREATE ROOM. You get a 5-letter code like V9DZN.
  • Your friend joins: opens your-site.netlify.app/multiplayer, types the code, taps CONNECT.
  • The match starts on both phones: creator is the home team, joiner is away.
  • One-tap rematch when the whistle goes. If your friend drops out, the computer takes over their team.

Controls — landscape, stick left, buttons right

TOUCH
  • Left thumb — the round stick moves your player
  • PASS — aim with the stick, tap to pass
  • SHOOT — hold to power up, release to strike; without the ball it's a tackle
  • ⚡ — sprint (stamina drains, then refills)
KEYBOARD
P1 WASD · F pass · G shoot · H sprint
P2 ↑←↓→ · J pass · K shoot · L sprint
Esc pause

What's in the game

  • Full 11 v 11 with real top-down players — boots, arms, shirts, squad numbers — 4-3-3 shape, goalkeepers, throw-ins, corners, goal kicks, half time, full-time stats.
  • Three ways to play: vs CPU (easy / normal / hard), 2 players on one phone (two pads on one screen), online by code.
  • 8 national teams, 3 / 5 / 8 minute matches, rematch in one tap.
  • Plays fully offline (vs CPU and 2-players). Online needs a connection — the phones talk directly to each other.

Tips

  • Aim with the stick while holding SHOOT to place your shot.
  • Get closer before you shoot — the keeper narrows the angle fast.
  • Sprint bursts beat defenders, but a tired player loses every 50-50.
  • Without the ball, the man nearest it is yours — press with him.
