HOW TO PLAY

Online

Main menu → ONLINE → CREATE ROOM gives you a 5-letter code. Your opponent opens /multiplayer on their phone, taps JOIN WITH A CODE and types it in. Host is the home team.

Touch controls

Left thumb = the round stick to move. Right cluster: PASS, SHOOT (hold to power up, release to strike), ⚡ to sprint. When you don't have the ball, SHOOT becomes a tackle/press. GK puts you in your keeper's gloves at any moment — move him with the stick, SHOOT to dive on the ball, PASS to throw it out. Tap GK again to give control back.

Keyboard (desktop)

W A S D / arrows move · J pass · K shoot (hold) · L sprint. Player 2: I J K L move · 1 pass · 2 shoot · 3 sprint. B grabs the keeper (P1), N for P2. V or the ⛶ button changes the camera.

Controls (4 buttons + stick)

Stick — run & aim. PASS — pass the ball; with no ball it switches you to the man nearest the ball. SHOOT — hold to power up, release to strike. RUN — hold to sprint (costs stamina). TKL — slide in and win the ball. Keyboard: WASD + F/G/H/B, or arrows + J/K/L/N.

Coins & the club

Every match pays coins: a win is worth far more than a loss. Spend them in CLUB · COINS & TRANSFERS on squad training (pace, shooting, passing, tackling, stamina) or to sign free agents who join your bench. Your career record lives in MATCH HISTORY.

LAN on a hotspot

No internet? One phone makes a hotspot, the other joins that wifi. In ONLINE → LAN · HOTSPOT, the host presses START and sends CODE A; the guest pastes it and gets CODE B back; the host pastes CODE B. The match then runs directly between the two phones.

Game speed

If the match feels frantic, set GAME SPEED to SLOW in the main menu (NORMAL is already slowed down a touch; FAST is the original pace). The match still lasts the same number of real minutes.

The view

Players stand upright on the pitch so you can see their faces, kits and how they strike the ball. The ⛶ button (or V) switches between whole-pitch, match and close-up cameras.

Formation & subs

TEAM SHEET (main menu, pause menu, or after the whistle) lets you pick a formation — 4-3-3, 4-4-2, 4-2-3-1, 3-5-2, 5-3-2 — and change the XI: tap a starter, then tap a bench player to swap them. In a match you get 5 substitutions; before kick-off and after full time you can change as much as you like. A sub comes on with full stamina. Your keeper always stays on.

Tips

· You control the man on the ball — or the nearest man to it. When the danger is in your own box, the keeper becomes yours: move him, then hit SHOOT to dive on the ball and PASS to throw it out.
· Aim with the stick while you hold SHOOT to place your shot.
· Pass into space: your teammates keep moving into position.
· Sprint burns stamina — it comes back when you slow down.
